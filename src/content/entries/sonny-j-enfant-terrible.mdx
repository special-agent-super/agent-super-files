For the better part of two years, a few seconds of French children's singing in Sonny J's **“Enfant Terrible”** (2008) refused to leave me alone.

It is a gloriously chaotic pop record, and the voices are just one small part of the commotion. Nevertheless, I wanted to know two things: what the children were singing, and where Sonny J had found the recording. I had an answer to neither.

In September 2026, I finally made some progress. I used **BS-RoFormer** to separate the voices from the music and **Whisper** to attempt a transcription, both through [MVSEP](https://mvsep.com/en). The result was imperfect, but for the first time I had something resembling actual French words to investigate. An AI-assisted search helped me straighten them out.

The crucial lines appeared to be:

> Qui volez toutes les pommes  
> En prison petits bonhommes

In other words: *You who steal all the apples - off to prison, little fellows.*

This was not quite what I had expected.

## A rather severe punishment

The trail led to **“Allez en prison,”** also known as **“Zon zon allez en prison,”** a traditional French children's song. [Chants de France](https://chantsdefrance.fr/repertoire/chants/allez-en-prison/) gives the same couplet, although the lines appear in the opposite order:

> En prison petits bonhommes  
> Qui volez toutes les pommes !

So there we have it. The offence is stealing apples. The penalty is imprisonment. No further details of the judicial process are supplied.

The song is described as a *ronde enfantine* - a children's circle game, passed down orally, with no known composer or established date of origin. That explains the words in Sonny J's record. It does not, unfortunately, identify the voices.

![Sheet music and lyrics for the traditional French song Allez en prison](/images/sonny-j-enfant-terrible/allez-en-prison-score.png)

<small>Sheet music and lyrics for “Allez en prison.” Screenshot from [Chansons-net](https://www.chansons-net.com/index.php?param1=EN00690.php).</small>

## The recording is another matter

There is at least one lead worth following. [Discogs](https://www.discogs.com/release/6287336) lists **Les Petits Chanteurs de Vincennes - “Zon Zon Allez En Prison”** on *55 Chansons Et Comptines (Et Plus) Pour Les Petits Et Les Grands Enfants*, a Disques Adès compilation (catalogue number **22017**, listed as **1970**).

An encouraging find. Unfortunately, a matching title is not a matching recording. I have yet to compare the audio on that compilation with the voices in **“Enfant Terrible.”** Until then, the Disques Adès record remains a possibility, not an identified sample source.

So, after almost two years, I can report the following: **the song is identified; the sampled performance is not.**

I am rather pleased with this outcome. A handful of mysterious syllables turned out to belong to a French children's game about apple thieves being sent to prison. It seems a perfectly good reason to keep listening.
